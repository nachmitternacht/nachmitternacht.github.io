    About

    nach.mitternacht — German for “after midnight” — is a small studio for architectural visualisation, based in Germany.

    The name stands for the quiet hours when the real work happens: the dedication to stay with a project long after midnight, until every detail is right.

    A visualisation here is more than a quick render. It starts with understanding the project, then slowly building a visual language, layer by layer, that tells the design exactly as it was meant.
